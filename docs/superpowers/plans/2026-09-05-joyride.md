# Joyride Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A polished browser open-world driving sandbox: walk, steal any car, drive, crash, wreck, get out, steal another.

**Architecture:** Data-Oriented Design. `src/balance.js` (config) and `src/gameData.js` (TypedArrays) are the contract and already exist. Logic modules in `src/logic/` are pure functions over `gd`; Board modules in `src/board/` render with three.js and stage input; `src/main.js` owns the single rAF loop with a fixed 60 Hz tick.

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES modules, three.js r185 vendored at `vendor/three.module.js` (importmap `"three"`), node:test for unit tests, puppeteer (already installed) for headless capture.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-joyride-design.md` (read it) and `GDD.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Logic modules (`src/logic/*`) import only `../balance.js` (constants/enums), never three, never DOM, never `Math.random` (use `gd.rand()`).
- No allocation in per-tick / per-frame paths: no object literals, closures, `.map/.filter`, `push`, `new` inside `tick()` or `render`-path functions. Module-scope scratch `Float32Array`s and reusable three.js `Vector3/Matrix4/Object3D` instances are the tool. Geometry rebuilds happen only on `carDirty` events (rare).
- Never rename or repurpose a field in `src/balance.js` / `src/gameData.js`. Adding fields (with a comment) is allowed.
- Coordinates: +X east, +Y up, +Z south. Yaw θ: forward `(sin θ, cos θ)` in (x,z). **Mesh-local: +Z = front, +X = left, +Y = up.** World→local: `lx = dx*cos θ - dz*sin θ` (left positive), `lz = dx*sin θ + dz*cos θ` (front positive). Left door world offset = `(cos θ, -sin θ) * d`. Lane right-hand offset for direction `(dx,dz)` is `(-dz, dx) * b.laneOffset`.
- Road centrelines at `x = -216 + i*54` and `z = -216 + j*54`, `i,j = 0..8`; node index `n = j*9 + i`; block `(bi,bj)` centre `(-216 + 27 + bi*54, -216 + 27 + bj*54)`, block index `bj*8 + bi`. Roads are 14 m wide, sidewalks 3 m, block interior 40 m, lot area 34 m.
- Events accumulate across ticks within a frame (`impCount`, `expCount`, `knockCount`, `carDirty`, `evtStolenCar`); Logic in a tick reads `[impTickStart, impCount)`; `main` calls `clearEvents(gd)` and zeroes `carDirty` **after** the Board has rendered.
- Colours in balance are `0xRRGGBB` numbers. No textures, no image assets, no external fonts. Everything must work offline from `npm start`.
- Every task: `npm run unit` must pass; commit at the end of each task with a message starting `feat:`/`fix:`/`test:`.
- Visual tasks must produce a screenshot with `node tools/shot.mjs` (Task 1) and look at it before reporting.

**Renderer (`src/board/renderer.js`)** — real implementation, not a stub:
- `createRenderer(canvas, gd, b, { headless }) → view` with `view = { renderer, scene, camera, sun, sunTarget, hemi, sky, clouds: Group, water, headless, tmp: {v1, v2, v3 Vector3s} }`.
- `WebGLRenderer({ canvas, antialias: true, preserveDrawingBuffer: headless })`, `setPixelRatio(headless ? 1 : Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 2))`, `outputColorSpace = SRGBColorSpace`, `toneMapping = NoToneMapping`, `shadowMap.enabled = true`, `shadowMap.type = PCFSoftShadowMap`. Resize on `window.resize`.
- `scene.fog = new Fog(b.colors.fog, b.sun.fogNear, b.sun.fogFar)`; `scene.background` = `b.colors.skyHorizon`.
- Sun: `DirectionalLight(b.colors.sun, b.sun.intensity)`; direction toward sun `(-0.75, 0.41, 0.52)` (unit-ish); `castShadow`; ortho shadow camera `left/right/top/bottom = ∓/±b.sun.shadowHalf`, `near 1`, `far 600`; `mapSize = headless ? shadowMapHeadless : shadowMap`; `bias -0.0006`, `normalBias 0.4`. Each frame set `sun.position = focus + dir*260`, `sunTarget.position = focus` where focus is the player/car position **snapped to a multiple of `2*shadowHalf/mapSize`** to stop shadow swimming. `HemisphereLight(b.colors.hemiSky, b.colors.hemiGround, b.sun.hemiIntensity)`.
- Sky dome: `SphereGeometry(900, 24, 12)` `BackSide` `ShaderMaterial` with uniforms `zenith`, `horizon`, `sunDir`; fragment: `mix(horizon, zenith, pow(max(vy,0), 0.55))` plus a warm sun glow `+ vec3(1.0,0.75,0.45)*pow(max(dot(dir,sunDir),0.0), 24.0)*0.55`; `fog: false`; `depthWrite: false`; centred on camera each frame.
- Ground: `PlaneGeometry(1600, 1600)` rotated flat, `MeshLambertMaterial(b.colors.grass)`, `receiveShadow`, y = -0.05.
- Hills: 28 flattened `IcosahedronGeometry(1, 1)` meshes (`flatShading`) scaled `(60–140, 18–45, 60–140)` on a ring radius 420–620 around origin excluding the south arc (water side, |angle from south| < 40°), colour `b.colors.hills` / `hillsFar` alternating, y = -8, seeded with a local mulberry32(99) — this is decoration, never gameplay.
- Water: `PlaneGeometry(1600, 700)` at `z = b.waterZ + 350`, y = -0.4, `MeshLambertMaterial(b.colors.water)`; a second thin plane strip `PlaneGeometry(1600, 6)` colour `waterDeep` at `z = b.waterZ + 3`, y = -0.35 (shoreline band). Animate: `water.position.y = -0.4 + 0.08*sin(t*0.8)`.
- Clouds: 10 groups, each 4–7 spheres (`SphereGeometry(1, 7, 5)`, `flatShading`, colour `cloud`, `fog: true`) scaled `(10–26, 5–9, 8–16)`, y 120–170, spread ±600; drift +x at 1.2 m/s, wrap at ±700.
- `renderFrame(view, gd, b, dtFrame)`: if `gd.camFree` use `camX..camLookZ` verbatim, else same fields plus shake: `camShake * (noise)` where noise uses `Math.sin(t*37)`, `Math.sin(t*51+1)` etc. (no allocation); `camera.fov = gd.camFov; updateProjectionMatrix()` only when fov changed > 0.01; sky follows camera; sun follows focus (`playerMode === IN_CAR ? car pos : player pos`); animate clouds/water; `renderer.render`.

**Produces:** `generateCity(gd, b)` fills: nodes/lanes (§5.1), blocks, render boxes, window bands, colliders, collision grids, props, parking spots. `nearestLanePoint(gd, b, x, z) → laneIndex` (closest point on any lane segment; writes `gd.scratch[0] = t` metres along that lane). `gridIndex(b, x, z) → cell index or -1`. `isOnRoad(b, x, z) → boolean` (inside any road strip: `|((x+216) mod 54) - 0| <= 7` handled with proper wrap, or same for z). **Lane graph:** for each node `(i,j)`, `nodeX = -216 + i*54`, `nodeZ = -216 + j*54`. For each horizontal road segment between nodes `(i,j)→(i+1,j)` create two lanes: eastbound from node A to B with right offset `(0, +3.5)` (so `z = nodeZ + 3.5`), and westbound B→A at `z = nodeZ - 3.5`. Vertical segments `(i,j)→(i,j+1)`: southbound `x = nodeX - 3.5`, northbound `x = nodeX + 3.5`. Lane endpoints are trimmed by `b.roadW/2` (7 m) from each node centre so they end at the intersection edge: `laneLen = 54 - 14 = 40`. `laneReverse` pairs each lane with its opposite. `nodeOutStart/Count/Lanes`: CSR of outgoing lanes per node (2–4 per node). Assert in the test that `laneCount === 288`, every node has ≥ 2 out lanes, every lane's `laneTo`'s out list excludes only its reverse when choosing.

**Blocks:** `blockRing = max(|bi-3.5|, |bj-3.5|) - 0.5` → 0..3 (ring 0 = central 2×2). Types: ring 0 → PLAZA; ring 1 → BUILDINGS (downtown, tall 18–48 m); ring 2 → BUILDINGS (midrise 9–20 m) with 2 seeded blocks → PARK; ring 3 → LOWRISE (5–10 m) with 3 seeded blocks → PARK. Never two parks adjacent (retry).

**Lots and buildings:** a block's lot area is the 34×34 square (block centre ± 17). Split: tall district → 1 lot (60%) or 2×1; midrise → 2×2 (60%) or 1×2; lowrise → 2×2 or 3×3 grid (each 11.3 m). Each lot: footprint inset by 1.5 m (lowrise 2.5 m), one building = stack of boxes: base box full footprint height h1; 40% chance second box inset 2–4 m on top with height 0.4–0.7·h1 (stepped); towers (ring 1, h > 30) get a crown: thin box 1 m tall slightly larger than the top, plus an antenna box `0.6×(6–12)×0.6`. Colour from `b.buildingPaint[district]` seeded; a 45% chance of a ground-floor band (box `w+0.3 × 3.6 × d+0.3` colour from `band`). Roof props: 1–3 small boxes (`1.5×1.2×1.5` or a tank `1.6×2.4×1.6`) colour `roofProp` at random roof positions for buildings ≥ 9 m. Window bands: for buildings with h ≥ 9 and a 65% seeded chance, one band per floor (floor pitch 3.2 m, starting at y = 4.4): box `w+0.12 × 1.1 × d+0.12`, written to `wb*` arrays (colour is always `buildingPaint.window`). Every base box also becomes a collider AABB with `colH = h1`. Landmark: block (3,3) (ring 0, NW) holds a tower `18×64×18` colour `landmark[0]` with a `landmark[1]` band every 6.4 m (window bands sized to it) and a 14 m antenna; the other three plaza blocks are open plaza (a render box slab `40×0.12×40` colour `plaza` at y=0.06 covering the block; the fountain prop at each block centre only for block (4,4); benches and trees around).

**Parks:** slab `40×0.1×40` colour `parkGrass` at y=0.05; 10–14 trees (TREE/PINE mix) at seeded positions ≥ 3 m apart inside the 34 m square; 4 benches; one FOUNTAIN at the centre (static collider circle via props).

**Sidewalk props:** along each block edge (4 edges of the 40 m block, sidewalk band 3 m wide), on the sidewalk centreline 1.5 m from the road edge: a LAMP at each block corner (offset 2 m inward on both axes) and at the edge midpoint; TREEs (ring ≥2) or PALMs (any block edge that faces the outer south road, `bj = 7` south edge) every 12 m offset ±0.4 m, skipping positions within 3 m of a lamp; one HYDRANT per edge at 30% chance; MAILBOX/BIN at 25% each; a cluster of 3 CONEs on the road edge at 6 seeded locations city-wide. `propScale` for trees 0.85–1.35, others 1. `propState = STATIC`, `propY = 0`.

**Waterfront:** along the south outer road (`z = 216`), promenade strip from `z = 223` to `240`: render box slab `(-256..256) × 0.12 × 17` colour `promenade` at y=0.06; PALMs every 9 m at `z = 232`; benches every 18 m at `z = 226` facing south; a pier: box `6×0.4×40` from `z = 240` to `280` at x = 0, y = 0.5 (colour `pier`) plus 8 post boxes `0.5×2×0.5` — decoration only (no collider; the world boundary keeps cars off it). Lamps along the promenade every 18 m.

**Parking spots:** along every road segment, on both sides, in the outer 2.6 m of the lane away from the centre (car centre at `roadCentre ± 5.4`), every 7 m starting 12 m from the intersection centre, yaw along the direction of travel of that side (right-hand traffic: the side at `+3.5` offset is travelled in the +direction). Skip any spot within 4 m of a CONE cluster. `parkUsed = 0`.
